## Evidence before story

Build a compact claim ledger: claim, exact quotation, precise locator, opened source URL, edition/textual version, evidence type, uncertainty, and verification status. Keep the quotation in context; it must actually support the claim, including the hook. Research specialized historical claims with primary or scholarly sources. Mark unresolved claims rather than inventing support.

Recompute sums and differences. Preserve a distinction between zero, absent, unknown, and included in another row. Align combined categories before counting disagreements. Record denominators and inclusions. Do not equate a gap in a list with missing people, theft, or vanished objects.
