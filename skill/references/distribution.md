# Distribution and iteration

Initial creative proposal, not a platform performance guarantee. Checked official guidance on 2026-09-10:
- TikTok: https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/creative-best-practices?trk=article-ssr-frontend-pulse_little-text-block
- Meta: https://www.facebookblueprint.com/student/page/584573-reels-creative-supercharger-march-19

These are advertising/creative training sources. Their vertical framing, sound and safe-zone recommendations can inform production; they do not establish organic ranking rules.

## One research topic, multiple entries

Use the HTML as an evidence-rich destination. Recompose one finding into a vertical video; do not shrink the whole webpage into a phone frame. Use a carousel for sequential discovery and a simple question/quiz for audience participation.

Starting production targets: video 1080×1920, 9:16, around 25–45 seconds; carousel 1080×1350, around 5–7 slides. These are editorial defaults, not claimed platform limits. Check current destination specifications and safe zones when exporting. Keep critical labels clear of interface overlays; do not assume a fixed safe-zone template works everywhere.

Open with a concrete puzzle. Show the evidence early, reveal the finding, then state what remains uncertain. Put a short source reference on the relevant frame, readable at phone size. Caption for muted viewing. Sound should support the story. Use one meaningful end prompt; never fake a controversy or hide a known explanation just to trigger comments.

Create separate Hebrew and English cuts; avoid two full languages competing in a single frame. Start with one repeatable visual system. Separate language accounts are an option if production capacity supports them; this is not a claim about algorithmic penalties for multilingual accounts.

## Small initial experiment

Over two weeks, test three topics with one video per language per topic, adapted to TikTok, Instagram Reels and optionally YouTube Shorts. This is six original cuts, not eighteen separately produced videos. Add a carousel if capacity allows. Do not require every topic to be a numerical contradiction: include cycles, movement, daily life and a surprising connection.

Track language and platform separately. Record reach/views as defined by that platform, average watch duration, completion where available, shares/reach, saves/reach, and profile or source visits. Record metric definitions; do not mix incompatible denominators. Compare within similar duration and account conditions. Early small samples are directional evidence.

Choose the next test from observed drop-off and responses. If the hook retains viewers but the middle loses them, simplify the evidence sequence. If people save/share, build a related follow-up. Change one major variable per iteration where practical. No universal target percentage or guaranteed posting time is assumed.
